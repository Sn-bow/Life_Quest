Life Quest: My Status Window

Know your next step. Complete guided quests and grow your own status window.

A status window for the things you actually do.
Life Quest turns a personal goal into a next action. Start with a topic you want to learn, a space you want to put in order, a comfortable break, or a connection you want to care for. You do not need to design a whole reward system before you begin.

FOUR ROUTES, A CLEAR NEXT STEP
Each route has 21 different missions across three chapters. Read the action, accept the mission, do it and leave an optional note. Your notes stay with the route so you can use what you discovered next time. The first 7 missions in every route are free: 28 guided missions in total.

SMALL COUNTS TOO
Busy day? Each mission has a small version with a concrete action. Use the optional focus timer, or work at your own pace. Time passing never marks a quest complete; you record what you did. Days off do not erase your route progress or subtract earned XP. The timer has no background alarm.

OPEN YOUR STATUS WINDOW
See your saved level, XP, title and four in-app stats. Complete a quest to see the actual reward and updated progress. These game stats are not measurements of your health or real-world ability.

LIFE QUEST COMPLETE — OPTIONAL ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Continue with missions 8–21 in all four routes: 56 additional missions, for 84 in total. Finish a route, keep its notes and start again with a new goal. Also includes three original status looks, 30/90-day reports from your completion history, and PNG, text and CSV report export. No subscription and no paid XP multiplier. Price is shown by Google Play before purchase.

YOUR OWN QUESTS REMAIN FREE
Create personal quests, get daily suggestions and choose whether to accept occasional surprise quests inside the app. Declining a suggestion costs no XP. Your existing history and password-encrypted backups remain free. Three short stories are an optional extra.

OPTIONAL ON-DEVICE AI
Core routes work without a model or account. On compatible Android devices, the optional Gemma 4 E2B download is approximately 2.59GB. Generation then runs on your device without an AI-call fee. Device performance varies; mobile data charges can apply. Review, edit or skip suggestions.

RECORDS AND PURCHASES
Start with a device profile without signing in. Back up your records before uninstalling; a forgotten backup password cannot be recovered. Buying or restoring requires a Google purchase account and internet. Verified paid access can be cached offline for up to 7 days before another online check. Connecting a purchase account does not upload your device-only goals or notes. An optional cloud profile is a separate choice.
