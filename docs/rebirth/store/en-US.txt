Life Quest: My Status Window

Open your status window. Turn small daily actions into quests and earn XP.

What if opening an app felt like checking your own status window?

Life Quest brings the personal status screen of modern fantasy stories into everyday life. Pick something you can do today—study for ten minutes, clear your desk, take a short break—and record it when you finish. The XP and in-app stats you earn appear on your status window.

YOUR STATUS COMES FIRST
The first screen shows your level, XP and stats. Finish a quest, see its reward, and return to a status window updated from your saved progress. A day off does not take away the everyday XP or levels you have earned.

QUESTS YOU CHOOSE
Get suggestions based on your available time, energy, interests and feedback on earlier quests. A suggestion becomes your quest only when you accept it. You can make and edit your own quests, too. Sometimes an unexpected quest appears inside the app when the conditions fit. Accept or decline it; declining does not reduce your existing XP or level. These surprise quests are not push notifications.

OPTIONAL, FREE ON-DEVICE AI
On compatible Android devices, you can choose to download the publicly available Gemma 4 E2B model. After installation, quest generation runs on your device, with no AI-call fee. The built-in suggestions and your own quests work without the model. The download is about 2.59GB; at least 3GB of free storage is recommended. Speed varies by device, and mobile data charges may apply. Review, edit or skip AI suggestions before accepting them.

MORE TO EXPLORE
There is also a card-based dungeon with branching paths and three free short stories shaped by your choices. Finishing everyday quests opens the next scenes.

STATUS WINDOW PLUS — OPTIONAL ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Unlock three original status looks and reports based on your recorded quest completions over the last 30 or 90 days. Review recorded XP, active days and streaks, then save the report as a PNG image, text file or CSV. Periods without enough recorded history are marked as incomplete. These reports reflect in-app records, not measured real-world ability. The pack adds no XP or stat boosts. The core status window, quests, existing history and encrypted backups remain free.

START WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Core use needs no account; progress is stored on your device. A free encrypted backup file lets you save and restore your records. Active dungeon expeditions are not included in backups. A purchase-only account connection is needed if you choose to buy or restore a purchase. Keep your backup password safe: it cannot be recovered if forgotten.
