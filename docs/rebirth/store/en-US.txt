Life Quest: Your Story

Your day opens three worlds. Personal quests, story choices and on-device AI.

Turn small everyday actions into quests that help you grow.

Life Quest is a habit and goal app with a fantasy status window. Choose an action you can do today, finish it, and see your experience and progress build over time.

THREE ACTIONS FOR TODAY
Set your available time, energy and interests. Recommendations adapt using completion, skipped quests and difficulty feedback. A suggestion becomes your quest only when you accept it. You can also create and edit your own quests.

PROGRESS THAT STAYS WITH YOU
View your level, XP, stats and history in your status window. Taking a day off does not subtract your everyday level or XP.

CHOOSE YOUR PATH THROUGH A CARD DUNGEON
Play attack and defense cards, then choose the next branch. Completed everyday quests and equipment support your expedition. Losing a battle does not reduce your everyday level or XP.

A STORY THAT OPENS WITH YOUR ACTIONS
Choose from three free short stories: the city mystery Exit Zero, the martial-arts practice story The Listening Courtyard, or the discovery story Atlas of the Missing Star. Everyday quests unlock new scenes and your choices shape later dialogue. Switch books without losing your choices or everyday progress.

OPTIONAL AI, RUNNING ON YOUR DEVICE
Install the public Gemma 4 E2B model on a supported Android device. After installation, generation runs locally: your goals and personalization history are not sent to a cloud AI service. There is no model-use or AI-call fee. Without the model, you can still use built-in recommendations and your own quests.

Model installation requires an approximately 2.59GB download and free storage space. Your mobile carrier may charge for data. Generation speed varies by device. Review suggestions before accepting them; you can edit or skip a quest.

START WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
The default mode stores your progress and personalization history on your device. Free encrypted backups let you save a file and restore it on another device. Keep the password safe: a forgotten backup password cannot be recovered.

Active dungeon expeditions are not included in backups. Expeditions are saved on the device when you enter or leave a room. After closing the app, an unfinished room restarts from its entrance.
